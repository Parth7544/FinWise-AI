LOANS AND DEBT MANAGEMENT

1. Understanding Loans
A loan is money borrowed from a lender that must be repaid over an
agreed period, usually with interest.

Important loan factors:
- Principal amount.
- Annual interest rate.
- Loan tenure.
- Monthly EMI.
- Processing fees.
- Repayment conditions.

2. Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI)
EMI is the fixed periodic payment made by a borrower toward repayment
of a loan.

EMI depends on:
- Principal loan amount.
- Monthly interest rate.
- Number of monthly instalments.

The EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)

Where:
P = Principal loan amount
r = Monthly interest rate in decimal form
n = Total number of monthly payments

3. Managing Loan Repayments
Responsible repayment practices include:
- Pay EMIs before their due dates.
- Understand the total interest payable.
- Avoid unnecessary borrowing.
- Maintain an emergency reserve.
- Review loan statements regularly.

4. Debt Repayment Strategies

Debt Avalanche:
Prioritise debts with the highest interest rates while maintaining
minimum payments on other debts. This can reduce total interest costs.

Debt Snowball:
Prioritise the smallest outstanding debt first while maintaining
minimum payments on other debts. This may help build repayment
motivation.

5. Credit Score
A credit score represents creditworthiness based on information in
a person's credit history.

Factors that may affect a credit score:
- Repayment history.
- Credit utilisation.
- Length of credit history.
- Credit mix.
- Recent credit enquiries.

In India, credit scores are provided by credit information companies.
A score should be interpreted according to the relevant provider's
scoring system.

6. Avoiding Excessive Debt
Good practices include:
- Borrow only what you can repay.
- Compare interest rates and loan terms.
- Avoid unnecessary credit applications.
- Pay bills on time.
- Monitor outstanding balances.

7. Financial Disclaimer
Loan calculations are estimates. Actual repayment amounts may vary
because of lender-specific interest calculations, fees, taxes, and
loan conditions.
