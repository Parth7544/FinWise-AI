BUDGETING AND EXPENSE MANAGEMENT

1. Understanding Expenses
Expenses are payments made for goods and services. They can be classified
into fixed, variable, and discretionary expenses.

Fixed expenses:
- Rent
- Loan EMIs
- Insurance premiums
- Internet subscriptions

Variable expenses:
- Groceries
- Electricity
- Transportation
- Medical expenses

Discretionary expenses:
- Entertainment
- Shopping
- Dining out
- Vacations

2. Expense Tracking
Expense tracking means recording and reviewing all financial
transactions.

Benefits include:
- Understanding spending habits.
- Identifying unnecessary purchases.
- Detecting unusual expenses.
- Improving monthly budgeting.
- Increasing savings.

3. Monthly Expense Analysis
Monthly expense analysis compares spending across different categories
and time periods.

Users should examine:
- Total monthly expenses.
- Category-wise spending.
- Changes compared with previous months.
- Recurring payments.
- Unusual increases in spending.

4. Expense Reduction Strategies
Practical ways to reduce expenses include:
- Set spending limits for each category.
- Compare prices before purchasing.
- Cancel unused subscriptions.
- Avoid impulse purchases.
- Plan grocery shopping.
- Review recurring payments regularly.

5. Spending Anomalies
An expense anomaly is a transaction that differs significantly from
normal spending patterns.

Examples:
- An unusually large shopping transaction.
- A sudden increase in electricity expenses.
- Duplicate payments.
- Unexpected recurring charges.

An unusual transaction is not automatically fraudulent. It should be
reviewed before taking action.

6. Expense Forecasting
Expense forecasting estimates future spending using historical
transaction data.

Historical monthly spending can help identify trends and estimate
future expenses. Forecasts are estimates and may change when income,
prices, or spending habits change.

7. Financial Disclaimer
Expense analysis and forecasts are estimates based on available data.
They should support financial planning rather than replace personal
judgement.
